The Pravahini Ritual

A Daily Flow, in Three Acts

pravahini — she who flows

Awaken

Morning: 3–4 drops of Silk Saffron Glow Serum on cleansed skin, sealed with Saffron Infused Cream. Two minutes, done mindfully.

Nourish

Weekly: the oiling ritual. Warm Botanical Hair Oil between your palms, massage into the scalp, let tradition do its work.

Restore

Evening: Fractionated Coconut Oil on damp skin after bathing — or as the base of a slow, restorative self-massage.

Our Story

Three women. One flowing vision.

mothers and grandmothers first, founders second

Pravahini — "she who flows" — began not in a boardroom, but around a family table. Our earliest memories of wellness come from home: a grandmother's hair oil warmed between palms, saffron saved for special days, remedies passed down like heirlooms.

"We make what we would trust on our own families — because we do use it on our own families."

Today it is hair, skin and body care. Tomorrow, wherever human wellness flows, Pravahini will flow with it.
