Avarampoo: the South Indian flower behind our bestselling cream

Senna auriculata — avarampoo in Tamil, tanner's cassia in English — has been part of South Indian skincare long before the word "skincare" existed. It's ground into nalangu maavu, brewed as a tea, and used in bathing powders across Tamil Nadu.

We infuse the flower extract into a shea butter and almond oil base, with lavender and rosemary oil.

  • Avarampoo flower extract, cold-infused
  • Shea butter and sweet almond oil base
  • Lavender and rosemary essential oils
